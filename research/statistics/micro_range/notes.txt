STRATEGY: Frozen Range Boundaries

Initially, I'll begin by trying to measure the unconditional behavior of every event:
What happens after an upper-bound close?
How often does it continue versus re-enter?
What is long/short MFE and MAE?
How does behavior vary by holding horizon?
How often does the midpoint or opposite boundary get reached?
My goal is to find whether the raw events contain any usable structure.
